What is Tic Tac Toe?

     

        Tic Tac Toe is a strategic 2-player game played on a 3×3 grid. Players take turns marking X or O in empty squares.         The first to align 3 of their marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins. This version uses AI to play against you.
